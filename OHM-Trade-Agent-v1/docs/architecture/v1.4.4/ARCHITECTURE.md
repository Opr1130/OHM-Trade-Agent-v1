2 Precision profit objective
O’Pip continuously searches for the strongest available opportunity to realize attractive risk-adjusted net profit over an efficient capital-occupancy period, subject to bounded and explainable downside risk, executable liquidity, evidence quality, portfolio constraints, and capital protection. It does not optimize for signal count or market participation. No trade is preferred when a sufficiently strong economic advantage is not established. [S5]
Economic interpretation
The governing objective remains MAXIMIZE SUSTAINABLE REALIZED NET PROFIT AFTER ALL COSTS. Ex ante selection uses calibrated expectations under approved constraints; realized outcomes evaluate whether those expectations were useful. Efficient capital occupancy is an explicit consideration, not a requirement to exit as quickly as possible or a replacement for portfolio net economics.
F7 compares eligible candidates and cash/no-trade on the same point-in-time panel. It records expected net economics, uncertainty, downside, liquidity, capital required and holding horizon. A profit-per-capital-time diagnostic may aid comparison only under a versioned policy with minimum evidence and duration rules. It must not favor a tiny, uncertain gain merely because its predicted duration is short.
Required distinction
Definition
Trading net
Realized proceeds less acquisition basis and recorded trading costs. Fees, spread, slippage and other execution costs follow the versioned accounting model; avoid double counting costs embedded in fill prices.
Operating net
Trading net less attributable AI, data, compute and operating costs. Unallocated or unknown costs remain explicit.
Capital occupancy
Integral of committed capital over elapsed time, including reservations as declared by policy. Report capital-hours and holding duration.
Expected value
Frozen forecast, horizon, uncertainty and cost assumptions at the decision cutoff; never a guarantee.
No trade
A valid ranked outcome when advantage, feasibility, calibration, evidence or risk conditions are insufficient.
Precision requirements
PR-01: No signal quota or minimum trade count may force selection. PR-02: Each actionable candidate needs a valid entry zone, expiry, explicit invalidation, realistic targets, bounded downside and execution feasibility at its declared notional. PR-03: Selection must record why it outranks the observed alternatives and cash. Claims are limited to the observed eligible universe.
After paper entry, approved deterministic protection and policy continue to assess thesis validity and remaining reward versus risk. Changing that policy remains a separate governed implementation. A faster exit is beneficial only if its measured economics and capital protection justify it.

4 Confirmation and contradiction
A discovered opportunity is not an actionable signal. Before operator presentation it must survive independent confirmation, contradiction search, execution/economic validation, portfolio-risk assessment and applicable advisory analysis using point-in-time eligible evidence. [S5]
The deterministic path remains discovery → candidate → timing and confirmation → contradiction checks → F5 feasibility → F6 forecast → F7 selection → ACTIONABLE / WAIT / REJECT. The Committee receives an asynchronous copy alongside this path. Its completion and stance are separate display fields, never runtime admission predicates.

Applicable structure, volume, momentum, volatility/regime, liquidity/order book and cross-market evidence. Correlated signals do not count as independent votes.
Contradiction

Presentation
Canonical disposition and current validity. AI review state and opinion remain separately labeled advisory metadata.
Applicability and missingness
Use all effective and applicable evidence sources, not every available source. The versioned evidence policy declares which inputs are mandatory for a setup and which are optional. Mandatory missing/stale/invalid evidence causes abstention or rejection. Optional missing evidence is visible and cannot count as confirmation. Source inclusion requires incremental-value evaluation, not persuasive anecdote.
A large on-chain transfer is an observed fact with attribution uncertainty. It does not by itself establish buying/selling intent or causal price impact. Separate transfer observation, entity attribution, economic interpretation and statistical evidence.
Expiry and requalification
REJECTED, NO_ACTION, LATE_EXTENSION, expired and missed-entry candidates cannot produce normal actionable trade alerts. Requalification creates a new point-in-time decision linked to the prior episode; it cannot reuse an old rejection or approval. Recheck validity at alert emission. If delivery consumes the remaining window, label the delivered state stale/expired using observed delivery evidence.
Confirmation has a bounded validity window. Late AI or backfilled evidence may create a separately timed later assessment; it cannot improve an earlier qualification or silently extend its expiry. Diagnostics and watch notices remain distinct from actionable trade alerts.

Fill/no-fill/partial fill; prices and costs; MFE/MAE; realized net P&L; time-to-profit; duration; exit reason; regime changes; protection interventions; later news/social/on-chain changes with their own timestamps.

EL-02: Maintain TAKEN, REJECTED, MISSED, EXPIRED, NO_FILL and NO_TRADE populations. Declare the evaluation universe, episode deduplication and horizon at registration. Outcomes are PENDING, MATURED, CENSORED or INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE with explicit reasons. A missing label is not a loss, win or zero return.
EL-03: Keep realized paper results, declared-policy counterfactual results and hindsight MFE/MAE separate. A rejected winner or no-trade evaluation uses a preregistered feasible policy and cost model. Hindsight best price is diagnostic and is never an achievable-fill claim.

A separately labeled Legacy Experience Corpus may use owner-provided Kraken history for execution and behavior analysis. It must not be mixed with clean prospective evidence or treated as reconstructed prior reasoning. Continuous learning creates candidates for improvement; operational behavior changes only through existing approval gates.

7 Causal confidence and attribution
Success and failure both require evidence-based attribution. No AI-generated post-hoc explanation may be recorded as proven causality without evidence. An observed fact may be proven while its causal contribution to the outcome remains unknown.
Category
Required standard
Example and limit
PROVEN
Direct trace or controlled evidence demonstrates the specified mechanism; scope and evidence references are explicit.
A recorded fee deterministically reduced ledger net by its amount. A late entry is measurable; that fact alone does not prove why the entire trade lost.
STRONGLY_SUPPORTED
Multiple consistent eligible observations, temporal order and a credible mechanism; alternatives assessed, residual uncertainty retained.
Execution deterioration is strongly supported by depth, spread and fill traces; unmeasured market effects remain possible.
HYPOTHESIS
Plausible explanation needing a registered test; no causal certainty implied.
Whale selling may have contributed to a reversal; entity intent and counterfactual price effect are not established.
UNKNOWN
Evidence is insufficient, contradictory or missing. Unknown is a valid final state.
Intrabar target/stop ordering cannot be recovered from the retained price data.
Attribution record
CA-01: Each claim stores claim identity, linked decision/outcome, outcome population, exact causal proposition, confidence category, evidence references and timestamps, mechanism, alternatives, contradictions, method/version, author or model provenance, reviewer, review state and supersession history. A numerical confidence score is optional and requires its own definition; it never substitutes for these categories.
CA-02: Separate fact confidence, source/entity attribution confidence and causal confidence. “BTC broke support before exit” can be an observed sequence without proving that it caused the loss. A high Committee agreement score cannot upgrade causality.
From explanation to improvement
A weakness record names its recurrence, economic impact and affected regimes. It links to a falsifiable hypothesis and preregistered experiment with a frozen comparator, population, cost model and stopping rule. Favorable anecdotes and a retrospective fit do not qualify for promotion.
Validation may confirm, reject or leave a finding inconclusive. Rejected and inconclusive findings remain visible. After an approved release, evaluate whether recurrence and economic harm actually declined on a new population while protection and reliability remained acceptable.
Avoid false precision
Error categories may overlap. Sum attributed economic contributions only when the decomposition method is identified and non-overlapping; otherwise report components separately. Unknown residuals stay visible. No retrospective explanation may leak into the original qualification, model input or prospective Committee comparison.

Measurement guardrails
A metric is not a pass threshold. None of these measures alone proves profit or causal improvement. Report cohort size, maturity, uncertainty and performance by regime, liquidity, side and independent time window. Timestamp uncertainty and unavailable path resolution restrict what can be concluded.
MFE/MAE are not executable returns; “best possible exit” remains hindsight. For unfilled or rejected candidates, use the registered reference and horizon, label the population, and keep hypothetical policy results separate from realized paper economics.
